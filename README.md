# Brothers by Rivalry — Part 1

An original visual novel. Two university boys, Aarav (loud, confident, stubborn)
and Kabir (quiet, loyal, stubborn), fall for the same girl, Meera. Their fight
turns into a bromance, they save her café's festival together — and then, at
the station, they go two different, separate paths. No one knows why.
That is the end of Part 1. Part 2 is coming.

- **Android:** package `com.orqa.brothersbyrivalry`, v1.0, landscape.
- **DEV-SIGNED ONLY** (test keystores in this folder) — sideload testing only,
  NEVER upload to Google Play; production needs a rebuild with the production key.
- Pictures and voices are AI-generated/synthetic — mark the AI-content
  declaration yes on any Play submission.
